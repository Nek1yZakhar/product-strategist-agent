# Synthetic Respondents & Persona Policy

This document defines the strict governance rules, authorized applications, and mandatory guardrails for utilizing LLM-simulated user personas and synthetic customer panels within the **Product Strategist Agent**.

---

## 1. Epistemic Guardrails & Scientific Honesty

Synthetic personas generated by Large Language Models can produce detailed and realistic qualitative narratives. However, academic research conclusively demonstrates that synthetic respondents **cannot reliably simulate human economic preferences, price sensitivity, or real purchasing decisions**:

- **No Statistical Demand Validity**: Citing [arXiv:2507.04438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04438) (*Chen, Zhu, & Zheng*), LLM agents suffer from severe *hypothetical compliance bias* and lack authentic financial constraints.
- **High Prediction Error**: Synthetic polling and simulated consumer behavior show substantial variance against real-world outcomes (e.g., Verasight 2026 validation study: RMSE 8.3 percentage points).

---

## 2. Permitted vs. Prohibited Use Cases

| Use Case | Permitted? | Methodological Rationale |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Exploratory Hypothesis Generation** | **YES** | Helps product leads brainstorm latent user anxieties and overlooked friction points. |
| **Qualitative Interview Guide Drafting**| **YES** | Generates probing questions and realistic objection scenarios for real customer interviews. |
| **Cognitive Blind-Spot Stress-Testing** | **YES** | Simulates edge-case user profiles (e.g., non-tech-savvy elderly relatives). |
| **Quantitative Demand Forecasting** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | Simulating market size or percentage adoption rates via LLMs produces pseudo-statistical slop. |
| **Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) Measurement**| **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | LLM personas do not possess authentic disposable income or budget tradeoffs. |
| **Substitute for Empirical Validation** | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** | Synthetic interviews can never substitute for real cohort analytics or live A/B tests. |

---

## 3. Mandatory Persona Output Disclaimer Banner

Any document, output file, or chat response containing synthetic respondent personas **MUST** be prefaced by the following standard banner:

```markdown
> [!WARNING]
> **METHODOLOGICAL DISCLAIMER: SYNTHETIC PERSONA SIMULATION**
> This respondent profile and dialogue is generated synthetically via LLM simulation for **qualitative hypothesis generation and blind-spot identification ONLY**.
> Under our scientific evidence standards (arXiv:2507.04438), synthetic responses **DO NOT** constitute empirical evidence of consumer demand, behavioral willingness-to-pay, or market validation. Real user verification remains mandatory.
```

---

## 4. Standard Persona Profile Schema

When generating synthetic personas for exploratory qualitative research:
1. **Demographic Context**: Age, occupation, household composition, location tier.
2. **Digital Literacy & Telecom Setup**: Primary operator, number of active devices, monthly telecom spend.
3. **Core Frustrations & Pain Points**: Current friction with telecom providers, hidden fears (loss of autonomy, billing opacity).
4. **Simulated Reaction to Concept**: Immediate qualitative objections, cognitive barriers, and spontaneous questions.
5. **Emerging Hypotheses**: 1–2 testable hypotheses extracted for live customer interview validation.
